CourtVision – free NBA stats and AI predictions

CourtVision is a free NBA analytics website with live scores, player and team stats, standings, and machine-learning predictions of every NBA player's next game. An XGBoost model trained on multiple seasons of game logs projects points, rebounds, assists and more, and picks the players most likely to outperform their averages on tonight's slate.
